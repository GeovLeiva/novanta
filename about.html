About Novanta Worldwide LLC

Novanta Worldwide LLC is an international trading and distribution company specializing in the sourcing, wholesale distribution, import and export of household appliances, machinery, equipment, hardware and general merchandise.

We work with suppliers and business partners across the United States and international markets to provide reliable sourcing solutions and a diverse range of products to our customers.

Our business connects suppliers with commercial customers, retailers and distributors, helping facilitate the purchasing and movement of merchandise across international markets.

With a focus on dependable service, competitive sourcing, and long-term business relationships, Novanta Worldwide LLC is committed to providing practical and efficient solutions for businesses of all sizes.

We serve customers in Belize, Mexico and Central America, with sourcing connections in the United States, China, and Panama.

Our Mission

To provide dependable wholesale distribution and international sourcing solutions by connecting businesses with quality products, competitive pricing and reliable service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted international trading and distribution partner, recognized for our commitment to customer satisfaction, reliable supply and long-term business relationships.
