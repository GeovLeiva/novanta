Our Services

Practical sourcing, distribution and international trade solutions for businesses.

Supporting Your Business Supply Needs

Novanta Worldwide LLC provides sourcing, wholesale distribution and international trade solutions designed to help businesses find the products and equipment they need.

Wholesale Distribution

We provide wholesale distribution solutions for businesses, retailers and commercial customers.


Our goal is to help customers source the products they need while offering competitive pricing and dependable service.

International Import & Export

We facilitate the sourcing and movement of merchandise between suppliers and customers in international markets.


Our trading network includes the United States, China, Panama, Belize, Mexico and Central America.

Product Sourcing

We assist customers in sourcing household appliances, machinery, equipment, hardware and general merchandise according to their purchasing requirements.


Customers can contact us with their product specifications, quantities and delivery requirements.

Machinery & Auction Sourcing

We source machinery, equipment and vehicles through established suppliers and auction platforms, including IAAI and other relevant auction houses.


Our sourcing services help customers identify available equipment based on their requirements and budget.

Business-to-Business Supply

We work with businesses, retailers, contractors, and commercial customers to help meet their purchasing and supply needs.


Whether customers require individual products or bulk orders, we aim to provide practical sourcing and distribution solutions.
